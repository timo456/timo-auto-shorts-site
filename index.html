From your library to your channel

A simpler daily routine.

01 / PREPARE

Keep the movement.

Scan local training clips, track previous selections, and export vertical H.264 / AAC videos. Local FFmpeg processing fits footage into a portrait frame while preserving the original source files.

02 / SOUND

Use music you can use.

Mix local, licensed tracks with original audio, rotate or randomize selections, and include recorded attribution in the description. A manual mode prepares clips for adding music yourself in the Shorts app.

03 / PUBLISH

Stay in control.

Generate numbered titles and descriptions, prepare batches, and upload through YouTube Data API v3. Uploads start private. Scheduling requires explicitly enabling Auto Publish and leaving test mode.

Your channel. Your permission.

Connect with Google OAuth.

The desktop app opens Google’s authorization screen, checks the authorized YouTube channel, and uses the granted permissions to upload videos, read their status, and set publishing times. It does not ask for your Google password.

The default schedule is 18:00 in Asia/Taipei. Once YouTube confirms a scheduled upload, YouTube handles publication. Preparing and uploading additional videos requires the Windows computer to be running.
